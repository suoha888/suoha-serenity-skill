# Research Output Contract

Every Quick, Standard, and Deep answer preserves the same semantics. The
amount of detail changes with the mode, not the integrity rules.

# Financial Translation
Revenue / profit / cash flow / funding / dilution, or UNKNOWN.

# Contradictory Evidence
Strongest alternative explanation:
Effect on the thesis:

# Unknowns and Next Checks
Unknown:
Exact resolving source or metric:
Next action:
~~~

Lead with a research judgment, not a ticker dump. A ranked company is a
research priority, never an automatic buy/sell instruction.

Every material claim must link to evidence IDs and show its time boundary.
Every unsupported relationship, stale input, inaccessible source, or failed
tool must be marked UNKNOWN, STALE, or NOT_ESTABLISHED.
