# suoha-serenity skill v4

See [README.md](README.md) for the full project guide.

suoha-serenity skill is an independent, evidence-first and time-aware
supply-chain and company research compiler. It tests architecture necessity,
records supply-chain edges and capacity states, separates company exposure from
economic capture and dilution, and turns a market narrative into a first-class
bottleneck assessment, dated company and market facts, a six-stage validation
ladder, a reflexivity audit, economic transmission, variant perception,
conditional research reasons, evidence, counterevidence, and a dated thesis
lifecycle.

The machine name is suoha-serenity-skill and the display name is
suoha-serenity skill. The project provides research support only: no trade
execution, return prediction, target-price promise, single composite conviction
score, or redistribution of third-party or subscription archives.
