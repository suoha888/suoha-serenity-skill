# Company Research Rubric v4

Score a report only when the relevant object is present and auditable.

| Dimension | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Identity | Legal entity, ticker, exchange, market, share class, and currency are resolved or explicitly UNKNOWN. |
| Company basics | Business, segment/revenue mix, customers, geographies, competitors, financial quality, and risks each have dates and evidence. |
| Market snapshot | Price, market cap, shares, source, quote type, basis, currency, and timestamps are separate. Stale/unavailable data is labeled. |
| Bottleneck | Architecture necessity, supply-chain edge, seven capacity states, and thirteen physical/economic dimensions are assessed separately; supplier count is not treated as proof. |
| Exposure / capture | Direct chain exposure, economic capture, and capital structure are separate dated assessments. |
| Translation | The selected business-model branch reaches revenue, margin, cash, funding, and dilution or names UNKNOWN. |
| Validation / reflexivity | The six validation stages are ordered; social-originated price action is not treated as independent fundamental evidence without confirmation. |
| Variant perception | Market proxy, overlooked mechanism, evidence, confidence, and disconfirming check are explicit. |
| Conditional case | Each research reason has IF/THEN/BECAUSE/CONFIRM/FAIL; no automatic buy/sell or return promise. |
| Integrity | FACT/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS/UNKNOWN and evidence state remain separate; no hindsight or access leakage. |
| Method application | Only applicable human-reviewed cards are listed; each card creates a company-specific question, and each supported/contradicted answer points to current evidence. |
| Thesis timeline | Relevant event IDs and dates are ordered after the current-evidence synthesis; past stance is not presented as current fact. |
| Report usability | Starts with a compact verdict card that separately states bottleneck existence, issuer position/economic capture, stock research priority, and valuation context; then follows the five-section layout. No repeated facts or unsupported hedge wording. Quick mode preserves the thirteen dimensions, seven capacity states, six-stage ladder, unknowns, and counterevidence. |

Release review must report the dimensions separately. A single composite
conviction score is a fail for this rubric.
